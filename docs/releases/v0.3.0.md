## Survey Toolkit 0.3.0 (beta)

**Your survey workspace.** Like the Microsoft 365 app, the Toolkit is now one window for your
tools.

- **A sidebar:** **Home**, with every tool and its updates, plus **Excel to KML**,
  **Traverse & Level** and **Slope Calculator**, which open right inside the Toolkit.
- **Already signed in.** The apps open with your Toolkit sign-in, so you never sign in twice.
- **They stay open** in the background when you switch, like tabs, and the last one you used
  comes back next time.
- **The app grid** button in the header, or **Ctrl+1 to Ctrl+4**, jumps between them.
- **Each app has** Back, Reload, Open in your browser, and **Windows app** when its Windows program
  is installed.
- **RoadPro and Project Folder Manager** stay on Home: RoadPro runs inside AutoCAD, and Project
  Folder Manager opens in its own window.

Signing out clears the apps' sign-in too.

The apps inside use Microsoft Edge WebView2, which comes with Windows 10 and 11.

## Survey Toolkit 1.1.0

**The Survey Toolkit is open to everyone.** Download it once from
[exceltokml.com/toolkit](https://www.exceltokml.com/toolkit), sign in with your free Excel to KML
account, and install Excel to KML, RoadPro, Traverse & Level, Project Folder Manager and Slope
Calculator with one click each. From then on every tool keeps itself up to date.

- **Background updates for every tool.** Excel to KML (from 4.4.3) and Traverse & Level (from
  2.1.1) now update by themselves when they aren't open, like the other three, instead of waiting
  until you open them from the Toolkit.
- **No more beta list.** Any free account can sign in. The "beta" labels are gone.
- Everything from the beta stays: the web apps side by side and already signed in, the tray and
  start with Windows, Automatic or Ask me for each tool, and every download checked by SHA-256.

**Already have a beta version?** It updates itself to 1.1.0 the next time you open it.

**"Windows protected your PC"?** The Toolkit isn't signed with a paid certificate yet. Press
**More info**, then **Run anyway**.

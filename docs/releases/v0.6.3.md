## Survey Toolkit 0.6.3 (beta)

**Excel to KML and Traverse & Level update in the background.** Until now the Toolkit could only
update these two when you opened them from the Toolkit, because their installers always opened the
program afterwards. Their new versions (Excel to KML 4.4.3 and Traverse & Level 2.1.1) can install
without opening, and the Toolkit now uses that: set to **Automatic**, they update by themselves
when they aren't open, like RoadPro, Project Folder Manager and Slope Calculator.

Until those versions are out, or for an older copy of the Toolkit, nothing changes: they update
when you open them from the Toolkit.

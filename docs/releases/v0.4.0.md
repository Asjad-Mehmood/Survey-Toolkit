## Survey Toolkit 0.4.0 (beta)

**Your tools now keep themselves up to date.**

- **Automatic updates.** When the Toolkit opens, and every 4 hours while it runs, it looks for
  new versions and installs them by itself, with the same official installer and SHA-256 check
  as the Update button. A Windows notification tells you, for example "Slope Calculator updated
  to 1.0.3".
- **Your choice for each tool:** **Automatic** or **Ask me**, in the new **Updates** section on
  Home. Nothing you haven't installed is ever installed by itself.
- **Never while you work:** a tool that is open is updated once you close it.
- **Excel to KML and Traverse & Level** open themselves after their installer, so they update
  when you open them from the Toolkit, just before they open.
- **Start with Windows and keep running in the tray** (off until you turn it on): the Toolkit
  starts quietly at sign-in and closing its window keeps it in the tray, so updates go on. Click
  the tray icon to open it; right-click it for **Check for updates now** or **Quit**.

Uninstalling the Toolkit also turns off Start with Windows.

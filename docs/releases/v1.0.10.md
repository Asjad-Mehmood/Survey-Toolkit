## Survey Toolkit 1.0.10

**Auto Draft is in the Toolkit.** The sixth tool: open the day's survey and Auto Draft joins the
lines by their field codes, places the symbols, writes the levels and draws the contours, then
saves a DXF for AutoCAD, a PDF on a sheet and a KML for Google Earth. Press **Install** on its card.

- **When Windows blocks an installer, the Toolkit says so plainly.** On PCs with Smart App Control
  (or a company's app policy) Windows can refuse an installer that isn't code-signed yet. The card
  now says what happened and what to do, instead of a technical error.
- **No more trying again and again.** A version Windows blocked isn't tried again by itself, even
  after a restart; pressing **Install** or **Update** tries it once more.

**Already have the Survey Toolkit?** It updates itself to 1.0.10 the next time you open it.

**"Windows protected your PC"?** The Toolkit isn't signed with a paid certificate yet. Press
**More info**, then **Run anyway**.

## Survey Toolkit 0.1.0 (beta, look only)

The first beta of the Survey Toolkit: one program that shows every survey tool from
exceltokml.com, what is installed on this PC and what is new.

- **Sign in** with your Excel to KML account. The beta opens for the accounts on the beta list.
- **Tools:** Excel to KML, RoadPro, Traverse & Level, Project Folder Manager and Slope Calculator,
  each with the version on this PC, the latest version and "Up to date" or "Update available".
- **Open** an installed tool, open its **web app**, or read **what's new**.
- **Look only:** this beta changes nothing on your PC. "Get it" and "Get the update" open the
  tool's page in your browser. Installing and updating from the Toolkit come in the next betas.
- The Toolkit updates itself when a new beta is published.

Windows 10 or 11, 64-bit. No administrator rights needed.

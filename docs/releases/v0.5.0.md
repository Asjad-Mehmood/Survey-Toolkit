## Survey Toolkit 0.5.0: the first beta

**All your survey tools in one program, always up to date.** A private beta: thank you for
testing it.

- **Home:** Excel to KML, RoadPro, Traverse & Level, Project Folder Manager and Slope Calculator,
  with what is on this PC and what is new. **Install**, **Update**, **Open** and **Uninstall**,
  each with the tool's official installer, checked by its SHA-256 before it runs. A tool that is
  open is never updated.
- **The apps inside:** Excel to KML, Traverse & Level and Slope Calculator open inside the
  Toolkit, already signed in. Switch with the sidebar, the app grid or Ctrl+1 to Ctrl+4.
- **Automatic updates:** when the Toolkit opens and every 4 hours. **Automatic** or **Ask me**
  for each tool, in the Updates section. A Windows notification says what was updated.
- **Start with Windows and keep running in the tray** (off until you turn it on).
- **It updates itself** too: you're asked once for each new version.
- **Your account:** the same as on exceltokml.com: email and password, or the browser (Google).

Windows may warn that it doesn't know the program, because it isn't code-signed yet: choose
**More info**, then **Run anyway**.

To remove it: Windows Settings → Apps → Survey Toolkit. Your tools stay installed; the Toolkit's
own settings and sign-in go with it.

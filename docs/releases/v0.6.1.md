## Survey Toolkit 0.6.1 (beta)

**Drop-down lists work inside the apps.** In the apps that open inside the Toolkit, a drop-down
list couldn't be opened, so a choice like Excel to KML's **Coordinates in my sheet are** (and the
grid steps after it) couldn't be made. Every drop-down now opens a list inside the page: pick with
the mouse, or with the arrow keys and Enter; Esc closes it.

Everything else is as in 0.6.0. The Toolkit offers this update by itself when it opens.
